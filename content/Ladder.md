Ladder is a ranking system for 1v1 found on [[DBDLeague]]'s Discord server, where players can challenge each other in 1v1 matches to climb up a leaderboard based on their performance.

## How It Works

On the [[DBDLeague]] Discord server, players challenge each other in custom matches.

Each player selects two killers to use across two [[Sets]]. A **Set** consists of two matches:

- In the first match, Player A plays as the killer while Player B is the survivor.
- In the second match, they switch roles.

The player with the better overall time across the two matches wins the Set. If both players win one Set each (1-1), a tie-breaker Set is played to determine the winner.

## Rules and balancing
There are a couple of very important channels on the Discord server, under the "dbd league ladder" category. It contains list of rules, balancing and killers. They make sure the gameplay is balanced for both sides.

## How to play
There's a very detailed, and official guide on the "1v1 basic rules" channel, found [here](https://discord.com/channels/459532023690821643/1205265326833672222/1351965864584548423) (make sure you've already joined the server in order to jump to the linked message).
