This wiki aims at providing up to date information about tournaments, teams and players, but also helping new fans understand the competitive scene of Dead by Daylight.


# Hi there!
Thank you for visiting my little website about competitive Dead By Daylight.

I'm Pazdikan, and I've been a fan of comp dbd since I've came across it, somewhere in late 2023... I know right, why am I even making a website for newcomers on a topic I'm also new to? Well because I want to help people like myself to learn more about this side of DBD without having to spend hours looking for information on everything.

When writing content for the wiki part of this website, especially for teams, I have to go through half of the internet just to learn more about the teams.

It's CRAZY HARD to find information on past comp dbd, especially as a new viewer. Discord server invites expire or channels are deleted. Teams' Discord servers are deleted or renamed, with channels purged ETC. This is why I'm committed to making this website.

## Accuracy of information
Well... as I stated before, I've been here for very little time and even when doing the research I'm not sure if - for example - a player is a temporary sub, or maybe he used to play for the said team, or maybe the team found someone that joined for a single match to prevent a forfeit?

When I finish the foundation of most teams, tournaments and players, I'll start asking people questions directly, so we can get less known and older information that is too old to be found on YouTube or Discord (e.g. deleted rosters, Pre 2021 data). 

Don't get me wrong, I could listen to what commentators and game analysts say, on the past 7 years of comp dbd events, but I don't have this amount of free time as the footage is in hundreds if not thousands of hours.
