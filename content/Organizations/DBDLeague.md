**DBDLeague** is an independent organization dedicated to competitive Dead by Daylight. Founded on June 23, 2018[^1], it has grown to become the biggest competitive community. On June 21, 2025, DBDLeague announced that they're officially supported by Behaviour Interactive[^2], the developers of Dead by Daylight.

# Developer Support
DBDLeague is _supported_ by Behaviour, not _sponsored_. Donations are still the main form of increasing prizes and cover organization and production costs.

Since there will be a LAN tournament in 2026, they provided DBDL organizers with a way to host their own server - which already is huge for competitive play. Other than that, the official [@DeadbyDaylight](https://x.com/DeadbyDaylight) X (formerly Twitter) account has started retweeting DBDLeague's posts. We can assume that Behaviour may make custom in-game cosmetics or give in-game currency for the tournament winners.
