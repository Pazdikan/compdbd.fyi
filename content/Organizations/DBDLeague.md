**DBDLeague** is an independent organization dedicated to competitive Dead by Daylight. Founded on June 23, 2018[^1], it has grown to become the biggest competitive community. On June 21, 2025, DBDLeague announced that they're officially supported by Behaviour Interactive[^2], the developers of Dead by Daylight.

# Developer Support
DBDLeague is _supported_ by Behaviour, not _sponsored_. Donations are still the main form of increasing prizes and cover organization and production costs. The official [@DeadbyDaylight](https://x.com/DeadbyDaylight) X (formerly Twitter) account has started retweeting DBDLeague's posts since the announcement. 

We can hope that Behaviour will make custom in-game cosmetics or give in-game currency for the tournament winners, but that's not confirmed just speculation!

# 2026 LAN
The 2026 LAN won’t use special servers built for the event. Instead, it will run on regular public servers, but everyone will have matching ping. It’s planned to take place in Frankfurt, which is where the German DBD server is located, so players should have around 15 -20 ms of latency.
